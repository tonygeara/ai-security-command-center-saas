# Edge Agent V11.9

## V11.9.0: one sign-in, and every feature on the client's licence

### One sign-in
- On a PC paired with the portal, the sign-in page also takes the **portal email and password**: the
  same account opens the website, the admin portal and the PC's dashboard.
- What you can do on the PC follows your portal role: super admin and company **Admin** → Admin,
  **Editor** → Operator, **Viewer** → Viewer.
- Checked by the cloud each time. If the internet is down, the last successful sign-in on that PC keeps
  working for up to 7 days (only a hash of the password is kept). Someone removed from the company, or
  blocked, in the portal is refused and signed out at the next check.
- Local accounts of the PC keep working as before; on-prem PCs use local accounts only.

### The licence covers every feature
- The provider can now switch on or off, per client, the newer features too: **camera-to-drone
  handoff, Smart patrol, drone-verified alarms, 4D incident replay and phone access** (admin portal →
  client → Feature access, or ticked on an on-prem licence).
- A feature switched off is turned off on the PC within a minute and can't be turned back on there.
  Clients never given the setting keep these features, as before. On-prem licences issued before V11.9
  keep them all.
- A suspended or blocked client gets every feature switched off.
