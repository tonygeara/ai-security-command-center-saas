# Edge Agent V11.5

## V11.5.0: one database, one event pipeline, incidents

### Incidents (new)
- **Settings → Incidents**: everything that happened at one camera, told as one story instead of
  scattered events: the first person or vehicle seen, unknown faces and plates (with snapshots), known
  visitors, the drone that was sent and what it did. Things at the same camera with less than 5 minutes
  of quiet in between belong to the same incident.
- Each incident has a level that only goes up: **activity** (people or vehicles), **unknown** (an
  unknown face or plate), **response** (a drone was sent). The list shows unknown and responses by
  default; operators and admins can mark an incident **reviewed**.

### One database
- Event history, sightings, the audit log and incidents now live in one SQLite database,
  `ai_security.db` in the data folder. It survives power cuts better than the old files (a cut while
  rewriting the CSV could lose the history) and makes questions like "how often has this car passed
  this week?" fast, which the next features need.
- On first start the old `event_history.csv`, `sightings.jsonl` and `audit_log.jsonl` are imported
  once and renamed `*.migrated` (kept, not deleted). Nothing changes on screen.
- The audit log is kept for a year; older records are pruned and the chain carries on from the first
  record kept.

### One event pipeline
- Camera detections, saved events, sightings and drone actions are published once on an internal
  pipeline; incidents and auto-dispatch now both listen to it, instead of each piece being wired to
  the camera code separately. Evidence packs and new alerts plug into the same place.

### Under the hood
- Sign-in, users and the first-run admin moved out of `dashboard.py` into `web_access.py`; the audit
  log, incidents, browser presence and shut down into `web_records.py`.
