# Edge Agent V11.17

## V11.17.0: Detector drones

- New **Detector drones** tab next to Drone: pin drone detectors on the map, each with **how far it detects**
  (a circle, 20 m to 10 km; drag a detector to move it, switch it on or off, rename or delete it).
- **Your drones are known; any other drone is an intruder.** When the first detector is created, every drone there is
  then (real and virtual) is registered under **My drones**. A drone added later is unknown until someone marks it
  as theirs, and a removed drone leaves the list (so a removed and re-added virtual drone is a new, unknown drone).
- **Alarm** when an unknown drone comes into a detector's range:
  - a red **UNKNOWN DRONE DETECTED** warning in the middle of every page, with a siren, until an operator or admin
    presses **Acknowledge** (anyone can silence their own screen); browsers play sound once the page was clicked;
  - a spoken warning and the warning sound on this PC, and a Telegram message;
  - the detection log on the Detector drones page, with distance, bearing and height.
- **Camera AI (YOLO)**: an admin uploads a YOLO model trained on drones (ONNX: YOLOv5, YOLOv8 or YOLO11); a detector
  given a camera then has its picture checked every 3 seconds, and a drone seen raises the same alarm, with the
  picture. Check the model's licence allows your use. Realistic camera range for a small drone: about 50–150 m (1080p
  wide), up to about 1 km with a zoom camera.
- Detection by position covers drones whose position this system knows (yours and virtual ones): used to test the
  whole chain with virtual drones. Other people's drones are seen by camera (or, later, a radio detector).
