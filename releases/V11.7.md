# Edge Agent V11.7

## V11.7.0: coverage map, camera-to-drone handoff, phone access

### Coverage (digital twin)
- **Settings → Coverage**: every camera's view drawn on the map (where it faces, how wide, how far),
  a heat map of the site (red: nobody watching, blue: one camera, green: two or more), the share of
  the site that is a blind spot, and the **best place for one more camera** with how much it would
  help ("blind spots would go from 80% to 64%").
- Draw the **site outline** by clicking its corners. Point a camera by clicking the map in the
  direction it faces, or type the numbers.
- **Click any spot**: see which cameras cover it, or **send a drone there**.
- **4D LIVE** shows the cameras and their views on the ground too.
- The views are drawn as open ground: walls, trees and hills in the way are not taken into account.

### Camera-to-drone handoff (off by default)
- When a person or vehicle **leaves a camera's view**, the nearest ready drone flies to where they
  probably went (worked out from where and which way they left the picture) and hovers there with
  its camera. It does not chase.
- Switch: **Drone page → Drone response → Camera-to-drone handoff**, or the Coverage page. It asks
  you to confirm, and follows the same people/vehicle choices, hours and cooldown as auto-send.
  Only cameras placed and pointed on the Coverage page take part.
- Telegram gets a message with a Recall button; the incident shows the handoff.

### Phone access (cloud edition, off by default)
- **Settings → System → Phone access**: incidents (unknown faces and plates, drone responses) with
  one snapshot each go to your portal account. Open **portal → Incidents & live view** on your phone.
- **Live view**: tap a camera on the portal page and the PC sends a fresh picture every 3 seconds
  while the page is open (pictures, not video). Nothing is sent while nobody watches.
- Nothing leaves the PC while the switch is off. The on-prem edition never contacts the cloud.
- Needs the new cloud parts (database tables, picture storage and the edge-sync function) to be
  installed on the server once.
