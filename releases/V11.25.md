# Edge Agent V11.25

## V11.25.0: Reports (daily, weekly, monthly)

- **Settings → Reports** (new tile in Settings, page `/reports`): choose a **daily**, **weekly** and/or **monthly** report,
  each with its time (and the weekday, or the day of the month: days 29–31 fall on the month's last day in shorter
  months), and what goes in:
  - **Detections:** people and vehicles seen, known people, unknown faces, number plates read, unknown plates, and
    unknown plates seen more than once (someone may be checking the place).
  - **Incidents and drone responses:** serious, medium and low incidents, how often drones were sent, the main ones.
  - **Drone flights:** flights, time in the air and distance per drone, flights flown despite the weather.
  - **Unknown drones:** alarms by kind (position, camera, Remote ID, acoustic, RF) and by detector.
  - **Verified alarms:** verified, marked false, sent to the monitoring centre.
  - **Cameras and PC:** how long the PC ran and how long each camera was offline.
  - **Busiest cameras and hours,** with a chart of the day.
  - Every number is compared with the period just before.
- Each report is sent to **Telegram** as a short message with a **PDF**, and kept on the PC for 13 months (list on the
  Reports page with Open and Download). **Preview** shows the report up to now; **Send now** makes and sends it.
- Reports are always sent, even with "Telegram: intruder warnings only" on. This also fixes the morning briefing, which
  that switch was silently blocking.
- **The daily report replaces the morning briefing.** Until the reports are first saved, the daily report follows the old
  briefing switch and time (on at 08:00 by default). The Drone page's "Send briefing now" button now sends the daily
  report.
- A report whose time passed while the PC was off is still sent when it comes back (daily: within 2 hours, weekly:
  2 days, monthly: 5 days); turning a report on after its time today does not send a late one.
- To count flights, unknown drones and offline cameras, the PC now keeps three small logs (drone flights from take-off
  to landing, unknown-drone alarms, and every 10 minutes the cameras that had no picture), kept for 400 days.
- Admins change the reports; operators and admins can press Send now; everyone can open the saved reports.
