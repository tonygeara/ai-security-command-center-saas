# Edge Agent V11.21

## V11.21.0: send drones to an unknown drone

- **Detector drones → "Send drones to an unknown drone"**: when a detector sees an unknown drone (by position, Remote ID,
  acoustic or RF sensors where its position is known, or a detector camera), your drones fly to it:
  - **which drones:** tick one or several of your drones; with none ticked, the nearest ready drone goes;
  - **where:** each holds a set distance from the unknown drone (40 m by default), on the side it comes from, at a set
    height (50 m by default; each further drone flies 5 m higher so yours keep apart), with its camera on it;
  - **following:** when the unknown drone moves 40 m or more, yours move with it (at most every 10 s);
  - **coming home:** when it hasn't been seen for a minute, after 15 minutes, or when someone presses **Recall**
    (one target or **Recall all**). They fly home round no-fly zones.
  - A drone seen only by a detector camera (no position) gets your drones to the detector, to look from there.
- **Automatic or by hand:** switch on **Send automatically**, or press **Send drones now** when the alarm goes off.
- Every flight goes through the usual checks (no-fly zones, weather, crewed aircraft, battery, licence); a drone that
  can't go is skipped and the reason is logged. The detections log, the drone's log, the audit log and Telegram (if on)
  say who was sent, by whom and when they came back.
- Operators and admins can send and recall; viewers can't.
