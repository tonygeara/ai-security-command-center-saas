# Edge Agent V11.12

## V11.12.1: version shown on every page

- The version and the edition (for example **V11.12.1 · ONLINE**, or **ON-PREM** for the on-prem edition) are shown
  at the top right of every page, under the clock, including the sign-in page. On narrow screens the clock hides and
  the version stays.

## V11.12.0: more DJI Dock control (look at, aim, temperature, orbit, emergency stop, siren and floodlight)

New buttons on a dock's card on the Drone page (operators):

- **👁 Look at**: click the map; the drone turns so its camera looks at that spot.
- **🎯 Aim**: click the drone's video; the camera points there.
- **🌡 Temperature**: click the drone's video; the thermal camera measures the temperature there, shown on the video
  and the card (an area reading with its hottest point is also available through the API). **Temperature off** ends it.
- **🌀 Orbit** (Dock 4): click the map; the drone circles that spot at its current distance and height with the camera
  on it. Refused if the circle would leave the dock's range or cross a no-fly zone, or is wider than 300 m.
  **Stop orbit** holds it where it is. Return to dock and Fly to a point end an orbit first.
- **⛔ Stop now** (emergency stop, with a confirmation): the drone stops and holds where it is. Dock 4 uses DJI's
  emergency stop; Dock 2/3 use DJI's stick-control emergency stop while keyboard control is on, otherwise they pause
  the route and stop any fly-to. Telegram is told who pressed it.
- **🚨 Siren** and **💡 Light** (any time the dock is online): the dock's sound-and-light alarm and its floodlight, on or off.
  The card shows "SIREN ON" while it sounds.
- Everything is in the activity log. The simulated docks answer all of these.
- Fixed: the drone's height on the Drone page is its height above take-off (DJI also reports an ellipsoid height,
  which was shown before on real docks).
- None of this has been tried on a real dock yet: see the new steps in `docs/DOCK-FIRST-FLIGHT.md` (section 8).
