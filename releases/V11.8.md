# Edge Agent V11.8

## V11.8.1: sign-in fixes

- **The sign-in page is styled again.** Once an account existed, the page's own stylesheet needed a
  sign-in too, so the page showed as plain text.
- **Forgotten dashboard password:** new Start menu item **"<product> - Reset sign-in"**. It opens a page,
  only in a browser on that PC and only for 15 minutes, where you choose a new password for an admin
  account. You are then signed in. The reset is recorded in the audit log.
- The sign-in page now says that the dashboard uses its own user names, not your portal email.

## V11.8.0: drone-verified alarms, 4D incident replay, Smart patrol

### Drone-verified alarms (off by default)
- **Settings → Monitoring centre.** When a drone is sent (auto-send, handoff, Telegram or the
  dashboard), it checks before anyone is called:
  - **verified by the drone:** once it arrives, its own camera sees a person or vehicle (within 60 s,
    adjustable);
  - **verified by a person:** otherwise Telegram asks "Is this a real intrusion?" (Real intrusion /
    False alarm), and the same buttons are on the Monitoring centre page;
  - **false alarms are never sent.** Nobody answering means not sent, unless you switch on
    "Send unconfirmed alarms too".
- A verified alarm goes to your monitoring centre in either or both ways:
  - **HTTPS webhook:** the site, camera, drone, place, who verified it and both pictures (camera and
    drone), signed so the centre can check it came from you. The signed evidence pack follows once
    the drone response ends;
  - **SIA DC-09 (Contact ID)**, the standard alarm panels use to report over IP to alarm-receiving
    centres: 130 burglary (verified), 380 (unconfirmed), 602 (test); the zone is the camera's number.
    Unencrypted: ask your centre for the address, account, receiver and line numbers.
- **Send a test** checks both before you rely on it. Every step is in the incident and the audit log.

### 4D incident replay
- **Incidents → open an incident → ▶ 4D replay.** The incident played back in 3D over the site: the
  cameras and what they see (a view lights up when it sees something), the drone flying its recorded
  path with a trail, a pin where it was sent, and each picture appearing at its moment.
- Play, pause, 1× to 60× speed, drag the timeline, or click a line in the timeline to jump there.
- Uses the 3D site map from 3D Maps when there is one within 3 km.

### Smart patrol (off by default)
- **Coverage page → Smart patrol.** The system writes its own patrol route: stops over the blind spots
  and the places where incidents happen (weighted by how serious and how recent), spaced apart, within
  the drone's range and outside no-fly zones, as a short loop from home.
- It flies every day at the hour incidents happen most (22:00 until there is history), and is rebuilt
  every week, with a Telegram note of what changed. **Rebuild now** on the page.
- Telegram asks Start / Skip at patrol time (as for any patrol), unless you switch on "Start without
  asking". Switching Smart patrol off removes its route and schedule.
- The route and its numbered stops are drawn on the Coverage map.
