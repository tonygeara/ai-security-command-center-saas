# Edge Agent V11.27

## V11.27.0: reports by email

- **Settings → Reports → Email** (PCs paired with the portal, online edition): tick who receives the reports. The list
  is your company's portal accounts (name, email, role), fetched from the portal; nobody else can be chosen. Each daily,
  weekly or monthly report (and Send now) is then also emailed: the same short summary in the email, the PDF attached.
- Sent by the portal (edge-sync v7, the same email service as the website's demo form). The portal checks every address
  is one of the company's portal accounts (someone removed from the portal stops receiving them and the PC says so),
  allows 12 report emails a day per PC, and refuses once the free trial has ended until a plan is chosen.
- If an email can't be sent, the report is still made, kept on the PC and sent to Telegram; the reason shows in the
  Send now message, on the Drone page's events and in the audit log (for example "email is not set up yet by your
  provider" when no verified sending address is configured).
- Only admins see and choose the people; on-prem PCs (no portal) have no email option.
