# Edge Agent V11.6

## V11.6.0: evidence packs, casing warnings, unusual activity

### Evidence packs (tamper-proof)
- **Settings → Incidents → open an incident → Download evidence pack.** One file for the police, an
  insurer or a court: the timeline, the camera snapshots, the drone's photo and video, the drone's GPS
  track during the response, and a report that opens in any browser.
- Every file's fingerprint (SHA-256) is listed in the pack and the list is **signed by this PC**. If
  anything in the pack is changed, removed or added, the check fails and says what.
- Anyone can check a pack: with the checker inside it (`verify_evidence.py`, instructions in
  `VERIFY.txt`), or on the Incidents page (**Check an evidence pack**). The page shows this PC's key
  fingerprint: give it to whoever receives your packs, so they know the pack came from your system.
- Making a pack is written to the audit log with the pack's fingerprint.
- During every drone response the drone's position is now recorded every 2 seconds for the pack.
- The PC's signing key (`evidence_signing.key` in the data folder) is made on first use. Keep it with
  your backups: a new key means new packs show a new fingerprint (old packs still check fine).

### Casing warnings
- When the same **unknown plate** comes by on **3 separate visits within 24 hours** (any cameras), you get
  one Telegram warning: "🔎 Possible casing: plate ABC123 came by 3 times… 21:02 (front), 23:40 (gate)…".
  It is also added to the camera's incident. Known plates never trigger it.
- **Settings → Patterns**: on/off, number of visits and hours.

### Unusual activity
- Each camera learns how many people and vehicles it usually sees in each hour of each weekday. When an
  hour has far more than usual (at least 5, and more than 3× usual + 3), you get one warning for that
  camera and hour. It needs two weeks of history first. **Settings → Patterns** to turn it off.

### Under the hood
- All Telegram alerts (unknown and known faces and plates, casing, unusual activity) now go through the
  event pipeline instead of being sent from the camera code.
- Fixed: the daily clean-up could fail on the first run after start (the audit log was not pruned).
