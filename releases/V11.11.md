# Edge Agent V11.11

## V11.11.0: DJI Dock 4 (battery swap)

- **DJI Dock 4 with the Matrice 5TD** can be part of a site, next to Dock 3 / Dock 2 and Drone Link. It connects
  through its DJI O4 Ground Station; the Drone page shows it as "DJI Dock 4" with the dock's own serial number.
- **Battery bays**: the dock card shows the charge of each battery in the dock's bays and how many swaps it has done,
  and "swapping the battery" while it swaps (about 25 seconds). The dock swaps by itself after landing below 79 %,
  so patrols and alarm launches can go again almost straight away.
- **🔋 Swap battery** (operators, drone in the dock): puts the fullest bay battery into the drone now. When a launch is
  refused because the drone is low, the message says which bay holds a charged battery.
- **Health alerts**: "low battery" now fires for a Dock 4 only when no charged battery is in the bays; a battery bay
  reporting a fault is a separate alert.
- **+ Simulated Dock 4** on the Drone page to try it without hardware.
- Dock 4 has not flown with this system yet: do the Dock 4 steps of `docs/DOCK-FIRST-FLIGHT.md` before using it for real.
