# Edge Agent V11.18

## V11.18.1: Detector drones: editing fixed, drag detectors and drones on the map

- Fixed: on the Detector drones page, changing a detector's **range**, switching it **on or off** and **deleting** it
  looked done but wasn't saved, and a **renamed** detector kept its old name in the list (the page's 2-second refresh
  left the buttons working on an old copy). All of them now save.
- **Drag detectors** on the map to move them (also when the drag lasts longer than the page's refresh).
- **Drag drones** on the map and drop them where they should go:
  - a **virtual drone**: **Fly there** (takes off and flies there) or **Move there now** (put there at once, to test a
    detector quickly);
  - a **real drone**: **Fly there**, after a REAL FLIGHT warning, with the usual checks (range from home, no-fly zones,
    weather, battery); it hovers there.
- The map now opens framing every detector **and** every drone.
## V11.18.0: Satellite and Detector drones are licence features, with Settings switches

- **Satellite view** and **Detector drones** are now per-client licence features, managed by the provider:
  - online clients: admin portal → client → **Feature access** ("Satellite view", "Detector drones"); on-prem: tick
    them on the licence (admin portal → Licences);
  - off for a client until switched on; the **Enterprise** plan includes both.
- **Settings → Tabs** on the PC: a switch for the **Satellite tab** and one for the **Detector drones tab** (admins).
  A tab shows only when the licence includes it **and** its switch is on; Settings says when the licence doesn't
  include it.
- A tab that isn't licensed or is switched off disappears from the top bar, its page says why, its data can't be
  read or changed, and Detector drones stops watching and raising alarms.
