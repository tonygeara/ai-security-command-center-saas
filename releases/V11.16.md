# Edge Agent V11.16

## V11.16.0: public live cameras worldwide on the Satellite tab

- Satellite tab → **📹 Live cameras**: public webcams anywhere in the world on the map (traffic, city, beach,
  harbour, ski and weather cameras), from the Windy.com webcam catalogue (over 60,000 cameras).
  - Move or zoom the map (country or city level) and the cameras there appear as markers and as a picture list,
    most watched first (50 at a time; zoom in for others). Red ● = a live stream, green ▶ = a timelapse of the day.
  - Click a camera to watch it in the middle of the page, with a link to its page on Windy.com.
  - Only cameras their owners publish are shown, never private CCTV that happens to be reachable online.
  - Needs a free Windy Webcams API key (api.windy.com/keys), added by an admin under Live cameras → Windy key; it is
    kept on this PC. The switch remembers its position on this browser.
- A new pin, or one saved without a day, shows **yesterday's** picture (today's is still being filled in, and is
  empty at night).
