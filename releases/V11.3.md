# Edge Agent V11.3

## V11.3.5: changing Wi-Fi or router
- **Every camera and recorder reconnects by itself** (before: only the main camera). Each device is recognised by
  its network card's fixed id (MAC) and found again at its new address with one shared network sweep; all moves
  are written in one update with one AI-service restart, and put back if that fails. Recorder (NVR/DVR) channels
  move together. A device that was never seen online on the old network is not searched for, so its password is
  never sent to unknown devices. Passwords with special characters (e.g. @) are now kept right when an address
  is rewritten (they were encoded twice before).
- **Drones reconnect without a new QR code** (needs **Drone Link 1.1** on the phone): after two failed attempts at
  the saved address the phone asks the Wi-Fi where the PC is (UDP 8766); only the PC holding that phone's pairing
  code answers, signed with it, and the phone saves the new address. Windows may ask once to allow the program on
  private networks: allow it.

## V11.3.4
- The place you look at is kept across the flat map, **3D MAP** and **4D LIVE** (and the other maps): a search,
  **◎ Locate me**, or moving a map or the 4D view saves it, and each view opens there.
- **4D LIVE** has the same **search** and **◎ Locate me** box: the 3D scene moves to the place (ground, zones and
  drones round it), marked with a pin.

## V11.3.3
- **Settings → Events / People / Vehicles** are pages of their own (tabs between them; **＋ Add person** and
  **＋ Add vehicle** there). They are no longer on the dashboard; old links to them still work.
- **Drone** tab: admins can **remove a drone** (virtual: deleted; an added drone: removed and its plan slot freed;
  the first drone, which always stays: unpaired, with a new pairing code). Not while it flies or is on a mission.
- Every map (Drone, 3D maps, Flight safety, Virtual drones) has an **address search** (OpenStreetMap; needs internet;
  coordinates like 33.89, 35.50 work offline) and **◎ Locate me** (this computer's location, or the site).

## V11.3.2: simpler navigation
- Top bar: Dashboard · Drone · Virtual drones · Devices · Settings.
- **Settings** is the hub for Events, People, Vehicles, Licence, Users and Flight safety, and has the switch
  **Camera live feed on the dashboard** (off: the main camera's live video is not shown or streamed there).
- **Drone** tab: flight safety in one row at the top (go / on hold, weather, aircraft, no-fly zones, link to its
  settings), and a **3D MAP** switch beside **4D LIVE** in the map panel that opens the 3D site-maps tool there.

## V11.3.1: installer fix
- Upgrading over a running copy no longer fails with "DeleteFile failed; code 5. Access is denied". Setup now
  stops the running program itself (it asks first, unless the install is silent) and starts it again when it is
  done. The AI services in Docker keep running. Every build now tests an upgrade over the running app.
- 4D live: click the ground to add route stops, or no-fly zone corners while drawing a zone, as on the flat map;
  the route and zone being drawn show in 3D (stops at their flying height). Click a drone's name below the view, or
  the drone itself, to fly the camera to it.

## V11.3.0

Windows customer runtime feature release.

## New
- **4D live** on the Drone page: switch the map to a live 3D view of every drone flying over the site, at its real
  position, height and heading, with its flight trail (last 15 minutes) and the route it is flying. No-fly zones,
  airports and airspace show as see-through volumes up to 120 m, crewed aircraft from the air-traffic watch at their
  height, and the latest finished 3D site map (3D Maps) is placed under the drones where it was flown.
  One switch per drone shows or hides it; **Follow** keeps the camera on a drone; **Whole site** goes back.
- 3D site maps now record where they are on Earth, so the live view can place them. Maps made with V11.2 need
  processing again (Process again) to appear there.
