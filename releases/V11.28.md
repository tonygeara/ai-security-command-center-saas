# Edge Agent V11.28

## V11.28.0: Add Robot

- **+ Add robot** next to + Add drone (Drone page and Dashboard) opens **Add a robot** (`/robot-add`): the robots that can
  be connected to the system, in groups:
  - **Robot dogs:** Boston Dynamics Spot, ANYbotics ANYmal, Unitree Go2 / B2, Ghost Robotics Vision 60,
    DEEP Robotics X30 / Lite3.
  - **Humanoids:** Unitree G1 / H1, Agility Robotics Digit, UBTech Walker S.
  - **Security patrol robots:** Knightscope K5 / K1, Cobalt Robotics, SMP Robotics S5, Ascento Guard.
  - **Hospital and service robots:** Aethon TUG, Diligent Moxi, temi, OhmniLabs Ohmni.
  - **Open ground robots:** any ROS 2 robot, ArduPilot / PX4 rovers and boats (MAVLink).
- Every robot is **on request**: choose it, give an email, company and details, and the request goes to your provider,
  who connects it with you (the robots are not driven by the system yet). Requests are listed on the page.
- **Others (find it automatically):** the PC listens on the network for about 6 seconds, sending nothing to the robot:
  - ROS 2 / DDS robots announcing themselves (Unitree and most research and service robots), with their node names;
  - ArduPilot / PX4 rovers, boats and submarines (MAVLink);
  - robots answering at their makers' usual addresses (Boston Dynamics Spot, Unitree's robot network). A network that
    answers for any address (a proxy or firewall) is detected and not counted, so nothing false is shown.
  - **Connect this robot** sends the request with what was found.
- Admins only (searching and requesting); everyone can see the list.
- The Add a drone list's MAVLink search now names ground rovers, boats and submarines instead of "aircraft".
