# Edge Agent V11.15

## V11.15.0: Satellite tab

- New **Satellite** tab, next to Dashboard: NASA satellite pictures of your places.
  - **Pins**: add as many named pins as you need (up to 50) by clicking the map, or search an address or "lat, lon"
    first. Rename, drag or delete them; they are saved on this PC (operators and admins change them, viewers look).
  - **Imagery** from NASA GIBS, free and with no key: one picture a day from the VIIRS (NOAA-20, afternoon) or MODIS
    (Terra, morning) satellite. Close to real time, not live: a day's picture is usually complete 3–24 hours later,
    and the detail is about 250–500 m (zooming in further only enlarges it).
  - **Day slider**: today back to 30 days ago. **View**: True colour, Fires (thermal anomalies seen that day) or
    Clouds (cloud-top temperature). Each pin keeps its own view, satellite and day.
  - **Fire alerts (optional)**: with a free NASA FIRMS key (Satellite → Fire alerts → FIRMS key, admins), fires
    seen within 50 km of each pin in the last 24 hours to 5 days are counted per pin and shown on the map, checked
    every 10 minutes. The key stays on this PC.
  - Needs internet on the PC's browser (imagery) and on the PC (fire alerts); on an offline PC the tab shows no
    pictures.
