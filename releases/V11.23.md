# Edge Agent V11.23

## V11.23.0: Fleet tab and Fleet card on the Dashboard: every drone on one map, with its controls

- **Fleet tab** (new, next to Drone): every drone on one map.
  - **Where each drone is, which way it flies and where it is going:** an arrow per drone pointing the way it flies,
    coloured by what it is doing (blue patrol, orange response, purple watching an intruder, yellow backup, white
    flying, green ready, grey offline); a dashed line to where it is going with the distance still to go; its route
    stops ahead; its patrol route (faded); and a trail of where it has been. Docks, no-fly zones and intruders being
    watched are shown too; offline drones are shown at their pin.
  - **A card per drone:** what it is doing (route and stop, camera it is answering, intruder it follows), distance to go,
    battery, height, speed and heading, and its controls: **Take off, Hover, Home, Land, Send to…** (click the map),
    **Patrol route** (draw or change its patrol route on the map: click to add stops, drag a stop to move it, set the
    height, speed and whether it repeats, then **Save** or **Save and fly now**), **Fly patrol, Stop route, Find** and
    **Open** (its Drone page).
  - **Move a drone by dragging it:** a virtual drone flies there or is put there at once; a real drone flies there after a
    REAL FLIGHT warning (usual checks); an offline drone moves its pin.
  - **For all drones:** filters (all, flying, ready, offline, low battery), the **Run patrol** switch, **Send ticked drones
    to a spot** (they spread out 20 m apart), **Stop all** (every flying drone hovers) and **Return all home** (patrols,
    responses and watches end; every drone flies home round the no-fly zones).
- **Fleet card on the Dashboard:** a small map with every drone and where it is going, a tile per drone (job, battery,
  distance to go), **Open Fleet page**, **Stop all** and **Return all home**. It is hidden when there are no drones.
- Every flight from the Fleet goes through the same checks as the Drone page (no-fly zones, weather, battery, licence).
  Operators and admins can fly; viewers can only look.
