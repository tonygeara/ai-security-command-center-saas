# Edge Agent V11.10

## V11.10.0: DJI Docks (DJI Cloud API)

A DJI Dock (Dock 3 with Matrice 4D/4TD, Dock 2 with Matrice 3D/3TD) can now be part of a site: its drone takes off
by itself, patrols, answers alarms and lands back in the dock. Drone Link (phone + controller) works as before next to it.
**Before using a dock for real, do the supervised first flight in `docs/DOCK-FIRST-FLIGHT.md`.**

### Connect and watch
- The dock connects to this PC over the site network (new local services on ports 1884, 8767, 8769). Setup from the
  Drone page: the DJI Cloud API keys and a link/QR code for DJI Pilot 2 on the dock's controller. Found docks are
  registered by an admin; a **simulated dock** is available to try everything without hardware.
- Docks appear on the Drone page (cards, fleet bar, map) with cover, drone, battery and weather.
- **Health alerts** (page and Telegram) when a dock goes offline, reports a cover fault or a DJI warning, sees heavy
  rain, strong wind or extreme temperature, or its drone is low and not charging, and when each clears.
- **Live video** from the dock's drone (wide, zoom, thermal) or the dock's own camera, through the same AI as the
  other drones.

### Flying by itself
- An admin switches **Automatic flights** on per dock (after confirming the site's permission) and sets its range.
- **Routes**: operators fly a saved route from a dock; it becomes a DJI flight plan the drone flies on its own, with
  checks before every take-off (licence, dock state, battery, warnings, wind/rain, range, no-fly zones, weather, air
  traffic), video on, progress on the map, **Return to dock**, and a Telegram report.
- **Alarm launches**: whenever the system sends a drone (auto-dispatch, handoff, Telegram, dashboard), the nearest
  ready dock can go. It asks **Launch / Don't** first unless an admin allows launching without asking. Drone-verified
  alarms work with docks.
- **Scheduled patrols** and the Smart patrol can be flown by a dock.
- **Live control** while it flies: fly to a point on the map, keyboard/gamepad sticks, photo, record, gimbal.
- **Photos and videos** the dock takes are saved on this PC and shown on the Drone page (kept 30 days); an alarm
  launch's first photo goes to Telegram.
- A crewed aircraft close by sends a dock's drone back to its dock.

### Licence
- New per-client switch **DJI Docks** and a dock count (admin portal: client → Feature access, licence form →
  "DJI Docks (max)"; on-prem licences carry them in the file). Off unless switched on.
- Switching a feature on or off in a client's Feature access keeps its limit (it used to clear it).
