# Edge Agent V11.22

## V11.22.0: Telegram only warns about intruders; the Drone page map always works, with Find drone, Pin drone and drag to move

- **Telegram: intruder warnings only** (Settings → System, **on by default**). Telegram now only gets:
  - a warning when an **intruder** is detected: an unknown person or vehicle, a confirmed intrusion, an unknown drone
    (Detector drones), and "person seen, drone sent" when a drone is sent automatically;
  - **questions that need your answer** (start a scheduled patrol? launch the dock? real intrusion?), because without an
    answer they'd be skipped;
  - **replies** to what you ask the bot (/status, questions, buttons).

  Known faces and plates, casing and unusual-activity notices, drone, route and patrol updates, patrol reports, the
  morning briefing (/briefing still answers), dock and system notices are no longer sent; they're still on the dashboard and in the logs. Switch it off to get everything again.
- **Drone page map** (each drone's page):
  - the map **always shows**, even when the drone isn't paired or has no GPS yet: at the drone, else its pin, the site,
    the last place you looked at, or the whole world, with address search and Locate me (it was blank before, so you
    couldn't find the drone, place it or draw a route);
  - **⌖ Find drone** centres the map on the drone, or says why it can't (not connected, no GPS yet); the map also jumps to
    the drone when its position first comes in;
  - **📍 Pin drone**: click the map where a drone is parked when it isn't sending its position. It's saved and shown as a
    dashed drone marker, routes are measured from it, and its live position replaces it once it's connected;
  - **drag the drone to move it**: a virtual drone flies there or is put there at once; a real drone flies there after a
    REAL FLIGHT warning, with the usual checks (no-fly zones, weather, battery); a pinned drone moves its pin;
  - the map is taller, and clicking it still adds route stops.
