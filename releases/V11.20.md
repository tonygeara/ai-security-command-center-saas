# Edge Agent V11.20

## V11.20.0: acoustic and passive RF drone sensors on Detector drones

- **Two more ways to detect drones** on the Detector drones tab, for drones that don't broadcast Remote ID and that
  cameras can't see yet:
  - **Acoustic sensors:** microphones that hear drone motors (also drones flying with no radio link, at night or
    behind trees; typically 100–300 m for small drones);
  - **Passive RF sensors:** receivers that hear drone control and video links (DJI OcuSync, ELRS, Crossfire, analogue
    video...), often as soon as the drone takes off and before it is seen (often 1–5 km).
  - Both only **listen**: nothing is transmitted or jammed.
- **How to set one up:** add a detector where the sensor is and set its **Type** to *Acoustic sensor* or *RF sensor
  (passive)*. The sensor (or a small computer next to it) sends what it hears to this PC as JSON, the same way as
  Remote ID receivers: by UDP to the same port, or by HTTP POST to `/api/sensors/report` with the receiver token
  (header `X-Sensor-Token` or `X-RemoteID-Token`). Example:
  `{"sensor": "North mast", "detected": true, "confidence": 0.82, "bearing": 210, "type": "quadcopter",
  "protocol": "OcuSync", "freq_mhz": 2440, "rssi_dbm": -61}`. Only `sensor` (the detector's name or id) is required.
- **What you see:**
  - a sensor that starts hearing a drone raises the **alarm** (red warning and siren on every screen, spoken warning on
    the PC, Telegram), with what it heard (type, radio link, frequency, signal strength, confidence) and the direction;
  - the map draws the **direction** each sensor hears the drone from (orange: acoustic, purple: RF);
  - where **two sensors' directions cross**, the drone is **located** on the map (or from one sensor that also gives a
    distance), and it counts in the range of any detector it is in;
  - a sensor hearing a drone in the direction of one of **your own flying drones** doesn't raise the alarm.
- **Settings:** "Alarm from confidence" (60% by default) on the Acoustic and RF sensors panel. Reports naming a sensor
  that isn't on the map are listed there so you can name a detector the same.
- **To try it without hardware:** `edge/tools/sensor_send.py` pretends to be two or more sensors and flies a test drone
  between them.
