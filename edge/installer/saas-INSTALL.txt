AI SECURITY COMMAND CENTER - INSTALLATION GUIDE (online edition)
==================================================================

Your provider has created your company account. You received:
  - the portal address:  https://www.aisecuritycommandcenter.com/login
  - your email and a temporary password (you choose your own at first sign-in)
  - the program:         AI-Security-Command-Center-Setup-<version>.exe

Video, faces, plates and recordings stay on your computer. The portal is used for your account,
your users, pairing the computer and (only if you switch it on) incidents on your phone.

COMPUTER REQUIREMENTS
  - Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, with virtualization enabled in the BIOS/UEFI
  - 1-2 cameras: 4 cores / 8 threads (Intel Core i5 8th gen / Ryzen 5 2000 or newer), 16 GB RAM
  - 4+ cameras: 8 cores, 32 GB RAM and an NVIDIA graphics card (RTX 3050 / 3060 or better)
  - SSD with at least 256 GB free (recordings: ~150-250 GB per camera)
  - Internet connection (it keeps working for up to 7 days if the internet drops)
  - IP cameras with RTSP video, 1080p or better, on the same network as the computer
  - Optional drones: DJI aircraft with the Drone Link app on the controller (ask your provider)

STEP 1 - SIGN IN TO THE PORTAL (5 minutes)
  - Open https://www.aisecuritycommandcenter.com/login and sign in with your email and the
    temporary password. Choose your own password when asked.

STEP 2 - INSTALL DOCKER DESKTOP (one time)
  Docker Desktop runs the local AI services.
  - Download it from https://www.docker.com/products/docker-desktop/ and install it with the
    default options (WSL 2). Restart the computer if asked.
  - Open Docker Desktop once and wait for "Engine running".
  - Settings > General > tick "Start Docker Desktop when you sign in".

STEP 3 - INSTALL THE PROGRAM
  - Run AI-Security-Command-Center-Setup-<version>.exe.
  - Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": click More info > Run anyway.
  - No administrator rights are needed.
  - The program starts with Windows and opens in your web browser. The first start downloads the
    AI services and takes several minutes.

STEP 4 - FIRST START: ADMIN ACCOUNT AND PAIRING
  - The browser asks you to create this computer's admin account (a user name and password).
  - Then it asks for a pairing code. In the portal, choose "Pair a Windows device": you get a
    one-time code, valid 15 minutes. Type it on the computer and click "Pair device".
  - Once paired, you can also sign in to this computer with your portal email and password.
    Portal Admins are admins here, Editors are operators (they can fly drones), Viewers can
    only watch.

STEP 5 - ADD CAMERAS
  - Dashboard > Devices > Add camera: scan the network to find your cameras, or enter the
    camera's RTSP address, for example rtsp://username:password@192.168.1.50:554/stream1
  - Recorders (NVR/DVR): add all channels at once from the same page.
  - The number of cameras and drones is set by your licence.
  - Camera passwords stay on this computer; they are never sent to the portal.

STEP 6 - ADD YOUR TEAM (portal)
  - Portal > Users > Add a user: give each person their own account.
      Editor: can change cameras, sites and computers
      Viewer: can see everything, change nothing
  - Each new user gets a temporary password, shown once: give it to them privately.

OPTIONAL
  - Drones: dashboard > Drone > + Add drone, then scan the QR code with the Drone Link app.
  - Telegram alerts with pictures and buttons: your provider sets them up with you.
  - Phone access (incidents and live pictures on your phone through the portal): dashboard >
    Settings > Phone access. Off until you switch it on.
  - Map your site and camera views: dashboard > Settings > Coverage.

UPDATES
  - When a new version is out, run the new Setup file over the installed one. Cameras, faces,
    events and settings are kept.

FORGOT A PASSWORD?
  - Portal password: ask your company Admin (Users > New password) or your provider.
  - This computer's own admin account: on the computer open Start > "AI Security Command Center
    - Reset sign-in" and choose a new password in the browser page it opens (15 minutes).

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Closing the browser does not stop protection. To stop everything: Settings > Shut down.
  - Motion recordings are kept 3 days, alerts and snapshots 14 days, event history 30 days.
  - Data folder: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AI-Security-System (back it up to keep faces and events).
  - Support: contact your provider.
